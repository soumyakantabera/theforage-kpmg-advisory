# Career Catalyst: Advisory - KPMG VEP Simulation

**KPMG Advisory Virtual Experience Program (VEP) – Full Simulation Portfolio**

This repository contains the complete deliverables from a 4-task KPMG Advisory simulation focused on **Healthy Co**, an urgent care provider with 25 facilities. The project demonstrates end-to-end consulting skills: client understanding, data analysis, leading practice research, executive presentation, and creative initiative brainstorming.

---

## Simulation Overview

**Client:** Healthy Co (25 urgent care facilities, 3,000 employees, 10,000 patients)  
**Core Challenge:** Improve patient experience and reduce long wait times, particularly during peak hours.  
**Approach:** Data-driven analysis (Task 2) + Evidence-based leading practices (Task 3) + Strategic recommendations and creative ideation.

The simulation follows the **KPMG CREATE Framework** for structured problem-solving and leverages Customer & Operations and Transformation service offerings.

---

## Task Breakdown & Deliverables

### 1. Understanding the Client's Operations & Reviewing Data
**Goal:** Analyze Healthy Co’s raw patient visit data to identify root causes of long wait times.

**Key Insights from Analysis:**
- Peak wait times >40 min at 9-10am, 12-1pm, and 6-7pm (up to 48.3 min at noon)
- 35% variation in nurse vitals time; Dr. Balla outlier at 29.6 min doctor wait ($1,482/visit cost)
- Group 3 patients (59% of volume) have shortest visits — ideal for Fast-Track
- Total average wait: 39.7 minutes across 4,984 visits

**Finalized Deliverable:**
- `Healthy_Co_Data_Analysis_Task2.xlsx` — Full data cleaning, calculated columns (Total Visit, Total Wait, Wait Costs), 9 pivot tables, summary tab, and charts

---

### 2. Leading Practice Research
**Goal:** Evaluate 5 curated resources and select the top 2 evidence-based strategies most relevant to Healthy Co.

**Selected Leading Practices:**
1. **Predictive Staff Scheduling** (National urgent care case study) — 15% wait reduction, 12% utilization gain
2. **Staff Retraining + Workflow Streamlining + Block Scheduling** (Peer-reviewed study) — 28% median wait reduction

**Finalized Deliverable:**
- `Healthy_Co_Task3_Completed.docx` — Resource evaluations, summaries, leading practice recommendations with rationale tied to Healthy Co data

---

### 3. Preparing a Summary Presentation
**Goal:** Synthesize Tasks 2 & 3 into a concise, executive-ready PowerPoint deck for Healthy Co leadership.

**Deck Structure (6 slides):**
1. Cover – Healthy Co Operational Efficiency Analysis
2. Executive Summary – Key insights + impact ($180-250K savings)
3. Data Insights – Peak hours, staffing variation, diagnosis group flow (with visual columns)
4. Leading Practices – Two evidence-backed strategies with fit to client
5. Next Steps – 3-phase 90-day implementation roadmap
6. Self-Reflection – Strengths, improvement areas, and key learnings

**Finalized Deliverable:**
- `Healthy_Co_Wait_Time_Reduction_Strategy.pptx` — Professional, KPMG-style presentation ready for delivery and recording

---

### 4. Initiative Brainstorming
**Goal:** Generate 10–15 creative, patient-centric ideas (ignoring constraints) and recommend additional KPMG service offerings.

**15 Initiatives** (High-impact examples):

- Real-Time Wait Transparency
- Text-to-Seat Virtual Queue
- AI Symptom Triage Chatbot
- Low-Acuity Fast-Track Lane
- Predictive Peak-Hour Staffing
- Tele-Urgent Care Diversion
- ... (full list in deliverable)

**Additional KPMG Services Recommended:**
1. Digital Transformation & Technology
2. People & Change / Human Capital
3. Customer Experience (CX) Transformation

**Finalized Deliverable:**
- `Healthy_Co_Initiative_Brainstorm_Completed.docx` — Fully populated template with impact/ease ratings and rationales
